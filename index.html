About

I work best where the product already exists, but the system behind it is still hard to see.

I start by understanding what is already there: the rules, constraints, old decisions, and gaps between teams. Then I make the next version clearer without pretending the product began with me.

I care about what remains after handoff — usable screens, explicit states, and documentation that helps Product, Dev, and QA make the same decision.
